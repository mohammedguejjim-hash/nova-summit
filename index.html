About the event

A summit for people who make things.

NOVA Summit gathers designers, engineers, and founders for two days of sharp talks and small-room workshops. No expo hall, no noise — just the ideas shaping how we build.

This year's theme, Design in the age of machines, asks what craft means when software can generate a thousand options before breakfast. Twenty speakers, six workshops, one city that loves to argue about the future.

Everything happens under one roof at the Austin Convention Center, with coffee that never runs out and hallways designed for the conversations that matter most.

  • 1,500+attendees

Why it’s different

Every talk is twenty minutes, every workshop is hands-on, and every speaker stays for the whole event. You’ll leave with notes, not just photos.
